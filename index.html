Are the programmes on YourDegree UGC-recognised?

Yes. YourDegree lists only UGC-entitled online programmes from recognised universities, such as D Y Patil, Chandigarh, Kurukshetra and O.P. Jindal Global University. Each programme shows the university’s NAAC grade so you can check it before you apply.

How does YourDegree help students choose the right course?

Speak to a certified counsellor, or talk it through with Disha, our AI voice counsellor. Both ask about your goals, your schedule and your budget, compare programmes side by side, and recommend a best fit with two more to consider.

Is YourDegree connected with recognised universities?

YourDegree is an online platform that helps students discover, compare and apply to online degree programmes offered by recognised universities across India.

Does YourDegree charge students for counselling?

No. Counselling calls are free. When you enrol, you pay the university directly, never YourDegree.

Are EMI options available for online degree programmes?

Many universities let you pay semester by semester, and several, including Chandigarh University Online and D Y Patil University Online, offer no-cost EMI. Use the Fee & EMI Calculator to see what a programme costs per month.
